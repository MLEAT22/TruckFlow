# Heavy Truck Garage Management System

# Workflow

## Heavy Truck Repair Workflow

### 1. Truck Arrival

* A truck arrives at the garage.
* The driver either comes directly to the garage or contacts the garage by phone before arriving.
* The driver explains the problems or repairs that need to be done.
* Daniel (Operations Manager) receives the truck.

---

## 2. Truck Registration

Daniel records the truck information on a prepared paper form.

Information currently recorded includes:

* Plate number
* Driver name
* Customer complaints (requested repairs)
* Date
* Daniel's signature

This paper becomes the truck's official repair record.

---

## 3. Initial Job Creation

Daniel reviews the requested repairs and decides which mechanics should perform each task.

For each repair:

* A mechanic is assigned.
* The repair task is written on a sticky note.
* The mechanic's name is written on the sticky note.
* The sticky note is attached to the truck.

At this stage, work officially begins.

---

## 4. Repair Process

Mechanics begin repairing the truck.

During the repair they may:

* Complete the assigned repair.
* Discover additional problems that were not reported by the driver.
* Need additional spare parts or materials.

When additional problems are found:

* The mechanic informs Daniel.
* Daniel decides whether the repair should be added.
* If necessary, Daniel contacts the customer for approval.

---

## 5. Spare Parts Request

If a mechanic needs parts or materials:

1. The mechanic informs Daniel.
2. Daniel writes the required materials on a purchase request paper.
3. The request is sent to Solomon (Owner).
4. Solomon reviews and approves the request by signing it.
5. After approval, the Procurement Assistant purchases the required materials.

Special case:

* If sheet metal ("lamera") is required, it is first taken to another workshop to be cut or bent into the required shape before being brought back to the garage.

---

## 6. Continue Repair

After the required materials arrive:

* Mechanics continue their repairs.
* Additional repair requests may still be discovered during the process.
* Daniel continues monitoring the progress of every truck.

---

## 7. Repair Completion

When all repairs are finished:

* The mechanics report completion to Daniel.
* Daniel verifies that all requested work has been completed.
* The truck is marked as ready for accounting.

---

## 8. Accounting

The repair record is delivered to the accountant.

The accountant:

* Calculates labor costs.
* Calculates spare parts costs.
* Calculates the total amount due.
* Prepares the final invoice.
* Records the transaction.

Some customers:

* Pay immediately after repair.
* Have monthly billing agreements.
* Pay through insurance companies.

Customer payment history must therefore be maintained.

---

## 9. Truck Delivery

After payment or according to the customer's payment agreement:

* The truck is released.
* The customer receives the repaired truck.
* The repair record is archived.

---

# Employee Payment Workflow

* Mechanics are paid weekly.
* Payment is mainly based on the amount of work completed.
* The accountant prepares payment information.
* Solomon approves payments.

---

# Current Problems

The current paper-based system creates several challenges:

* Difficulty finding old repair records.
* Difficult to know who worked on a truck previously.
* Difficult to know the current status of every truck.
* Difficult to know which trucks are delayed.
* Difficult to know which mechanic is working on which truck.
* Managers frequently walk around the garage to check progress.
* Mechanics rely on Daniel for most information.
* Spare parts requests are tracked using paper.
* It is difficult to know whether ordered parts have arrived.
* Customer history is difficult to search.
* Managers rely heavily on memory and verbal communication.

---

# Future Digital Workflow

The software should allow Daniel, Solomon, the accountant, and other staff to:

* Register incoming trucks digitally.
* Create digital job cards.
* Assign mechanics digitally.
* Track every repair task.
* Track spare parts requests.
* Monitor truck progress in real time.
* Identify delayed trucks before they become major problems.
* View the current status of every truck.
* Search any truck instantly.
* Record customer history.
* Record supplier purchases.
* Calculate invoices.
* Track employee work for payroll.
* Generate reports for management.
